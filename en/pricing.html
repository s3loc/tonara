Questions

How pricing works

Is the health check really free?

Yes. We review the current state and send the main findings in writing. It takes one business day and is not billed.

Why can the final price differ from the starting price?

Data volume, number of queries, current setup, and downtime needs can change the scope. We itemize the difference in the quote.

Will we know the total before work starts?

Yes. The work, timeline, and total price are set out in the written quote. There are no extra charges unless the scope changes.

Can we start small?

Yes, and it is how we prefer to work. We can start with a single group of queries, one backup flow, or a limited RAG scenario.
